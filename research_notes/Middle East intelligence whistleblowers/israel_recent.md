## Key Question 1: The Sde Teiman video leak and Military Advocate General Maj. Gen. Yifat Tomer-Yerushalmi (2024-2026)

### Takeaway
Israel's top military lawyer admitted on 31 Oct 2025 that she had authorized the August 2024 leak to Channel 12 of surveillance footage of reservists abusing a Palestinian detainee at Sde Teiman, saying she did so to counter "false propaganda" against military law enforcement; she was arrested two days later on suspicion of fraud/breach of trust, abuse of office, obstruction of justice and unlawful disclosure (plus suspected lying to the High Court), was dismissed by the IDF chief on 26 May 2026, and as of early October 2026 still had not been indicted, with the case frozen by the Supreme Court pending a request for a further hearing and plea talks reported but unconfirmed. The soldiers' own indictment was withdrawn on 12 March 2026. Israeli officialdom and most mainstream Israeli/US outlets framed her as a leaker who obstructed justice; some international and left-leaning commentators called her a whistleblower.

### Cited Findings

**The underlying incident and the video**
- The alleged assault took place on 5 July 2024 at the Sde Teiman detention facility in the Negev; the detainee was reportedly cuffed at hands and ankles and blindfolded during a "search" by reservists of Force 100, a Military Police unit responsible for high-risk security prisoners — [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/news/israel-indicts-five-reservists-over-alleged-prisoner-abuse); [JNS](https://www.jns.org/idfs-top-lawyer-drops-sde-teiman-affair/)
- The military said the abuse caused cracked ribs, a punctured lung and an inner rectal tear; another outlet describes the ribs as broken and says the rectal tear required intestinal surgery — [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7462562); [Yahoo/Telegraph: "badly beat Palestinian prisoner for 15 minutes"](https://www.yahoo.com/news/israel-charges-five-soldiers-badly-155105819.html)
- On 29 July 2024 Military Police detained nine reservists who guarded the facility — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/idfs-top-lawyer-drops-sde-teiman-affair/)
- In August 2024 five soldiers accused of gang-raping the detainee were released to house arrest under a 10-day agreement between the military prosecution and defense lawyers — [The New Arab](https://newarab.com/news/israelis-accused-raping-gazan-detainee-put-house-arrest)
- Channel 12 broadcast the surveillance footage in August 2024; it showed a detainee lying on the floor while soldiers surrounded him with riot shields blocking the view of how he was treated; the IDF said the video had been "misleadingly edited" — [CNN explainer, syndicated via KVIA, 3 Nov 2025](https://kvia.com/news/us-world/cnn-world/2025/11/03/a-leaked-video-a-frantic-search-and-the-top-military-lawyer-what-to-know-about-the-scandal-rocking-israel/); [CNN, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/04/middleeast/major-general-tomer-yerushalmi-israel-intl)
- The reservists claimed in court the recording had been doctored using footage from two separate days — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/idfs-top-lawyer-drops-sde-teiman-affair/)
- Background: three Israeli whistleblowers who had worked at Sde Teiman described abuse to CNN in May 2024 (amputations due to handcuffing, wounds left to rot, beatings), and a month later authorities said they would begin shutting the facility — [CNN via KVIA](https://kvia.com/news/us-world/cnn-world/2025/11/03/a-leaked-video-a-frantic-search-and-the-top-military-lawyer-what-to-know-about-the-scandal-rocking-israel/); [Mediaite on CNN report](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/whistleblowers-tell-cnn-palestinian-prisoners-being-tortured-and-beaten-in-israeli-prison/)

**The soldiers' indictment (Feb 2025) and its withdrawal (Mar 2026)**
- On 19 Feb 2025 five of the nine reservists were indicted for causing severe injury and abuse under aggravating circumstances (aggravated assault/aggravated injury); investigators relied on medical documentation and surveillance video — [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/news/israel-indicts-five-reservists-over-alleged-prisoner-abuse); [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7462562)
- On 12 March 2026 the new MAG, Maj. Gen. Itai Ofir (also spelled Offir/Itay), cancelled the indictment, citing "significant developments" since filing that affected "both the prosecution's ability to establish the charges at the level required in a criminal trial, and the ability to ensure that a fair trial is conducted" — [IDF official statement](https://www.idf.il/en/mini-sites/military-advocate-generals-corps/the-military-advocate-general-s-decision-to-withdraw-the-indictment-against-the-force-100-soldiers-from-the-sde-teiman-base/); [CNN, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/12/middleeast/israel-charges-dismissed-sde-teiman-intl); [JNS](https://www.jns.org/idfs-top-lawyer-drops-sde-teiman-affair/)
- Reasons reported: the detainee had been released to Gaza in October 2025 (apparently as part of the hostage deal) without his testimony being taken; the leak scandal meant the former prosecution team itself was under criminal investigation; the prosecution invoked the "defense of justice" (fair-trial) doctrine — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/idfs-top-lawyer-drops-sde-teiman-affair/); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-897350); [Lawfare, "End of the Sde Teiman Abuse Case"](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/end-of-the-sde-teiman-abuse-case--the-idf-mag-withdraws-indictments)
- ABC (Australia) reported military legal officers said the leaked video did not show violence serious enough to support a conviction and had been improperly leaked — [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-03-13/israel-idf-drops-sde-temain-reservist-palestinian-abuse-case/106449540)
- JNS reported a medical opinion suggested a self-inflicted wound (single source; treat with caution) — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/idfs-top-lawyer-drops-sde-teiman-affair/)
- Yesh Din said the cancellation "proves that Israel is unwilling and unable to prosecute soldiers suspected of serious violations of international humanitarian law" — [Al Jazeera, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/12/israel-drops-charges-on-soldiers-who-allegedly-raped-palestinian-detainee)
- The IDF said a command inquiry into the Force 100 soldiers had not yet been conducted and did not bar them from returning to duty; the former suspects were later reinstated as reservists — [JNS via Cleveland Jewish News](https://www.clevelandjewishnews.com/jns/former-suspects-in-sde-teiman-affair-reinstated-as-reservists/article_94c22ed3-7ebb-591c-be33-f1e2842efaae.html)
- On or about 1 Sept 2026, three of the soldiers (assisted by the Honenu legal aid group) sued 17 individuals and institutions for NIS 18 million, including Tomer-Yerushalmi, Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara, Channel 12 reporter Guy Peleg, the IDF, Military Police and Israel Police, alleging wrongful indictment and defamation — [VINnews, 1 Sept 2026](https://vinnews.com/2026/09/01/3-soldiers-from-sde-teiman-base-sue-prosecutors-for-18m-nis-over-wrongful-indictment/); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-907146)

**The leak probe, cover-up allegations and her admission**
- A second criminal investigation was opened in late 2024 into how the footage reached the media and whether senior officials broke confidentiality laws or abused authority — [CNN via KVIA](https://kvia.com/news/us-world/cnn-world/2025/11/03/a-leaked-video-a-frantic-search-and-the-top-military-lawyer-what-to-know-about-the-scandal-rocking-israel/)
- An Attorney General's Office statement that investigators could not find the leak's source — now understood to be false — was submitted to the Supreme Court in September 2025 as part of a state response; a report by the deputy MAG (Asael) to the Supreme Court and Knesset stated the leak did not come from the MAG corps but that "hundreds of people were exposed to the materials"; Army Radio later reported Asael said he did not know the leak came from within the corps and he is not a suspect — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ex-idf-legal-chief-sent-to-house-arrest-as-her-missing-phone-is-possibly-found-in-the-sea/); [Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2025/11/idf-advocate-general-yifat-tomer-yerushalmi-sde-teiman-prison-abuse/)
- On Wednesday 29 Oct 2025 the IDF suspended her the same day AG Baharav-Miara announced a criminal investigation into the leak — [JPost, "IDF suspends chief lawyer"](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-872059); [CNN via KVIA](https://kvia.com/news/us-world/cnn-world/2025/11/03/a-leaked-video-a-frantic-search-and-the-top-military-lawyer-what-to-know-about-the-scandal-rocking-israel/)
- Two days later (31 Oct 2025) she resigned, acknowledging she approved the release and taking "full responsibility for any material that was released to the media from within the unit"; her tenure as MAG ran 1 Sept 2021 to 31 Oct 2025 — [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/sjemwlmjbg); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/israel/israels-military-prosecutor-admits-leaked-video-soldiers-assaulting-pa-rcna241233); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yifat_Tomer-Yerushalmi)
- Her stated reasons: the release was "an attempt to counter the false propaganda directed against the military law enforcement authorities"; she described an "incitement campaign" against her that peaked after the decision to investigate the Sde Teiman soldiers, said legal officers faced personal attacks and threats, and stressed the military's legal and ethical duty to investigate unlawful acts even in war — [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20251103-israel-arrests-ex-military-legal-chief-who-leaked-video-showing-abuse-of-palestinian-detainee); [CNN via KVIA](https://kvia.com/news/us-world/cnn-world/2025/11/03/a-leaked-video-a-frantic-search-and-the-top-military-lawyer-what-to-know-about-the-scandal-rocking-israel/); [Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2025/11/idf-advocate-general-yifat-tomer-yerushalmi-sde-teiman-prison-abuse/)
- Katz "issued no fewer than seven statements against the chief military attorney in less than a week," accusing her of a "blood libel" against the soldiers "even though the video's authenticity had not been disputed" — [The Week](https://theweek.com/world-news/israel-arrest-idf-chief); [Israel National News](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/432310)

### Inferences
- The case is legally unusual: the person who leaked is not accused of espionage or endangering state security but of integrity offenses (breach of trust, obstruction, false affidavit). The gravest allegation in the eyes of prosecutors and the courts is the alleged cover-up (the misleading state response to the High Court and the discarded phone), not the disclosure itself; this is why Israeli coverage uses "leaker/cover-up" rather than "whistleblower."
- Her own justification is a classic public-interest defense (countering disinformation that abuse was fabricated, protecting investigators), yet she was the senior official responsible for the investigation and had lawful channels, which weakens the whistleblower analogy in Israeli commentary.
- The collapse of the soldiers' prosecution means the leak's net effect, as of Oct 2026, was that the alleged abusers face no criminal case while the leaker remains under investigation, which is the point stressed by Yesh Din, The Conversation and +972.

## Key Question 2: The "Bild leak" / classified documents affair — Eli Feldstein and Ari Rosenfeld (2024-2026)

### Takeaway
A Military Intelligence reservist NCO (Ari Rosenfeld) passed classified Hamas documents to Eli Feldstein, a spokesman in Netanyahu's office, who leaked one to Germany's Bild (published 6 Sept 2024) to shape the Israeli hostage debate; both were arrested 27 Oct 2024 and indicted 21 Nov 2024, Feldstein on the life-imprisonment count of transferring secret information with intent to harm state security; Netanyahu adviser Yonatan Urich was added by amended indictment in June 2026; as of October 2026 the Tel Aviv District Court trial has not begun and pleas are due 15 Dec 2026. ## Key Question 3: Unit 8200 insiders and the Gaza targeting/surveillance disclosures (Gospel, Lavender, Microsoft Azure)

### Takeaway
Anonymous current and former Israeli intelligence personnel gave +972 Magazine/Local Call (and later The Guardian) three major disclosures: "The Gospel" AI target-generation system (30 Nov 2023, seven sources), "Lavender" (3 Apr 2024, six sources) and Unit 8200's storage of an estimated 200 million hours of Palestinian phone calls on Microsoft Azure (6 Aug 2025, 11 sources plus leaked Microsoft documents). The IDF denied the Lavender characterization while not denying the database's existence, and an Israeli official first called the Microsoft arrangement "legally supervised" then denied any IDF data work with Microsoft; Microsoft disabled the relevant Israeli Defense Ministry subscriptions on 25 Sept 2025 and closed its review in June 2026 with findings unchanged. The sources have remained anonymous; I found no evidence of them being identified, prosecuted or formally recognized as whistleblowers, though international commentary (e.g., the Ellsberg Award blog) frames them that way.

### Cited Findings

**"The Gospel"/Habsora (30 Nov 2023)**
- +972/Local Call's "A mass assassination factory" was based on conversations with seven current and former members of Israel's intelligence community (military intelligence and air force), plus Palestinian testimony and IDF statements; officials confirmed on condition of anonymity the existence of "Habsora" ("The Gospel"), which the IDF had briefly announced in early November 2023 — [+972 Magazine](https://www.972mag.com/mass-assassination-factory-israel-calculated-bombing-gaza/)
- Source quotes: a former target-division official called it part of a "mass assassination factory" where "the emphasis is on quantity and not on quality"; "We work quickly and there is no time to delve deep into the target... we are judged according to how many targets we manage to generate"; "When a 3-year-old girl is killed in a home in Gaza, it's because someone in the army decided it wasn't a big deal for her to be killed" — [+972 Magazine](https://www.972mag.com/mass-assassination-factory-israel-calculated-bombing-gaza/); [Futurism summary](https://futurism.com/israel-ai-targeting-hamas)
- Sources said the army holds files on most potential targets, including homes, stipulating the number of civilians likely to be killed, and expanded strikes on "power targets" (residences, public buildings, high-rises) intended to "create a shock" that pressures civilians to pressure Hamas — [+972 Magazine](https://www.972mag.com/mass-assassination-factory-israel-calculated-bombing-gaza/)

**"Lavender" (3 Apr 2024)**
- Published 3 April 2024 by Yuval Abraham in +972/Local Call, with parallel Guardian reporting; based on six Israeli intelligence officers who served during the war and were involved in using AI to generate targets, speaking anonymously — [+972 Magazine](https://www.972mag.com/lavender-ai-israeli-army-gaza/); [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2024/apr/03/israel-gaza-ai-database-hamas-airstrikes); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/israeli-military-lavendar-ai-gaza-rubber-stamp-report-1851384474)
- Key claims: Lavender marked about 37,000 Palestinians as suspected militants; human review took about 20 seconds per target ("rubber stamp"); the unit judged it ~90% accurate (10% error); pre-authorized civilian-casualty allowances of 15-20 (reported in some summaries as 5-20) for junior operatives and up to 100 for senior commanders; a companion system "Where's Daddy?" tracked targets to their homes; the army "almost completely relied" on Lavender in the early weeks — [Yahoo/Times of Israel summary](https://au.news.yahoo.com/israel-reportedly-used-lavender-ai-005212961.html); [WION explainer](https://embed.wionews.com/world/explained-israeli-militarys-use-of-ai-lavender-to-generate-kill-lists-707558); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/israeli-military-lavendar-ai-gaza-rubber-stamp-report-1851384474)
- The report tied the concept to a 2021 book by "Brigadier General Y.S.," identified as the then-commander of Unit 8200, advocating a machine to generate targets rapidly in war — [Yahoo summary](https://au.news.yahoo.com/israel-reportedly-used-lavender-ai-005212961.html)
- IDF response to The Guardian (3 Apr 2024): "Contrary to claims, the IDF does not use an artificial intelligence system that identifies terrorist operatives or tries to predict whether a person is a terrorist"; the "system" is "simply a database whose purpose is to cross-reference intelligence sources"; "Information systems are merely tools for analysts in the target identification process"; analysts "must conduct independent examinations... in accordance with international law"; the output "is not a list of confirmed military operatives eligible to attack" — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2024/04/03/idf-denies-uses-ai-software-target-individuals-gaza-bombing-campaigns/); [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai/es/reports/3851)

**Microsoft Azure / Unit 8200 (6 Aug 2025 to June 2026)**

### Inferences

### Inferences
- These letters are political dissent, not whistleblowing: no secrets were disclosed and the signatories asserted a policy judgment. The IDF's sanction (dismissal from reserve duty) and the government's "fringe" framing mirror the 2014-15 Unit 8200 precedent but on a far larger scale.
- The intelligence-community pedigree of signatories (8200, Mossad, Talpiot) lent the letters authority on the security question, which is why the government responded so sharply.

## Key Question 5: Other 2023-2026 insider disclosures connected to intelligence, detainee abuse or October 7 (including the Ronen Bar dispute)

### Takeaway
Besides the headline cases, the period produced: the November 2023 "Jericho Wall" revelations built on leaked Unit 8200 emails showing an analyst's ignored warnings; a Sde Teiman field-hospital doctor's April 2024 letter to the AG and ministers about handcuff-induced amputations; a Shin Bet reservist lieutenant colonel ("Aleph") arrested in April 2025 for leaking the agency's internal October 7 assessment and a probe of Kahanist influence in the police to Minister Amichai Chikli and journalists, whom the right celebrated as an "Israeli hero"; and Shin Bet chief Ronen Bar's April 2025 sworn affidavit alleging Netanyahu demanded surveillance of protesters and loyalty over the courts, which preceded the High Court's 21 May 2025 ruling that his dismissal was unlawful. The "Aleph" case is the closest analogue to a right-wing whistleblower claim and was defended on public-interest grounds; Bar's affidavit is a lawful disclosure through a court, not a leak.

### Cited Findings

**"Jericho Wall" and leaked Unit 8200 emails (Nov 2023)**
- The New York Times (30 Nov 2023) reported Israel obtained a roughly 40-page Hamas attack blueprint code-named "Jericho Wall" more than a year before 7 Oct 2023; officials dismissed it as "aspirational," and a Gaza Division assessment treated it as a "compass" of Hamas ambitions — [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel-at-war/1701415006-israel-dismissed-intel-about-hamas-attack-plan-over-a-year-ago-report); [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-news/israel-hamas-attack-knew-over-year-1234906803/)

## Key Question 6: Legal context — the Military Censor, gag orders, Penal Law provisions, and whether any whistleblower-protection argument has succeeded

### Takeaway
The Gaza war produced record censorship (1,635 articles fully barred and 6,265 partially redacted in 2024, about 21 interventions a day, 38% of 20,770 submissions), heavy use of court gag orders that were repeatedly contested and partially lifted, and prosecutions under the Penal Law's secret-information provisions (life imprisonment for transfer with intent to harm state security; seven years for unauthorized possession). In none of the 2023-2026 cases reviewed did a court accept a public-interest or whistleblower defense; the only successes for leak defendants were procedural (interim findings of insufficient evidence on intent, releases to house arrest, gag orders lifted).

### Cited Findings
- The Military Censor is a unit inside Military Intelligence; under Israeli law any article dealing with the broadly defined category of "security issues" must be submitted for review; the High Court ruled in 1989 that the censor may intervene only when publication would bring a "near certainty that real damage will be caused to the security of the state" — [+972 Magazine, "Breaking new records"](https://www.972mag.com/israeli-military-censor-media-2024/); [Columbia Journalism Review](https://www.cjr.org/feature/israeli-military-censor-unit-idf-intelligence-corps.php)
- 2023: the censor barred 613 articles and partially redacted 2,703 (highest redaction figure since 2014); 2024: 1,635 fully blocked and 6,265 partially redacted out of 20,770 submissions (about 21 interventions per day, a 38% intervention rate), the most extreme since +972 began collecting data in 2011; 2025: 753 fully barred and 4,974 redaction demands, the second-highest year on record (data from a freedom-of-information request with the Movement for Freedom of Information) — [+972 Magazine](https://www.972mag.com/israeli-military-censor-media-2024/); [+972 Magazine, "15 articles"](https://www.972mag.com/15-articles-israeli-army-media-interference/)
- Al Jazeera explainer on how Israel restricts media reporting (June 2025) — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/6/19/how-does-israel-restrict-its-media-from-reporting-on-the-iran-conflict)
- The Bild document itself had been barred by the censor to protect a Gaza source — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/with-gag-order-lifted-reservist-charged-in-pms-office-leak-case-named-as-ari-rosenfeld/)
- Gag orders in the cases above: Feldstein case gag partially lifted by a Rishon Lezion court (Nov 2024) after Netanyahu and media organizations requested removal; Rosenfeld's name released by the Tel Aviv District Court with other details still barred; the Shin Bet "Aleph" gag (imposed 6 Apr 2025) lifted by Judge Menachem Mizrahi because partial information was already circulating — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/netanyahu-media-organizations-request-removal-of-gag-order-on-pmo-security-affair/); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/court-permits-lifting-gag-order-on-name-of-idf-reservist-in-secrets-leak-case/); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.com/2025/04/14/shin-bet-police-probe-new-security-case-all-details-under-gag-order/)
- Penal Law provisions: a person holding secret information without authorization and without intent to harm state security faces up to seven years under section 113(c) of the Penal Law (as cited in the Uri Blau precedent); transfer of secret information with intent to harm state security carries life imprisonment (the count against Feldstein); Rosenfeld's "providing secret information" counts carry seven years — [RSF (Arabic) on Uri Blau](https://rsf.org/ar/%D8%A3%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%88-%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%87%D8%A2%D8%B1%D8%AA%D8%B3-%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B1-%D8%B6-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%B9-%D8%B3%D9%86%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/with-gag-order-lifted-reservist-charged-in-pms-office-leak-case-named-as-ari-rosenfeld/); [JPost](http://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-902484)
- The Tomer-Yerushalmi suspicions are integrity offenses (fraud and breach of trust, abuse of office, obstruction of justice, disclosure of information by a public official) rather than espionage provisions — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/former-idf-legal-chief-military-prosecutor-to-stay-in-detention-over-sde-teiman-leak/)
- The prosecution in the Feldstein case explicitly argued that the leaker's motive "is not the basis for the charges" and invoked foreseeability of harm, foreclosing a public-interest defense — [Times of Israel liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/prosecutors-issue-qa-document-on-pmo-documents-leak-case-to-combat-misinformation/)
- The only judicial wins for leak defendants were interim/procedural: Judge Masarwa's finding of insufficient evidence on Feldstein's intent-to-harm count at a detention hearing; Rosenfeld's and Tomer-Yerushalmi's releases to house arrest; the "Aleph" judge finding no grounds for detention on dangerousness given his cooperation — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/tel-aviv-court-finds-insufficient-evidence-to-prosecute-eli-feldstein-for-intending-to-harm-state-security/); [JNS](https://www.jns.org/ari-rosenfeld-suspect-in-pm-leak-affair-released-to-house-arrest/); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.com/2025/04/15/details-emerge-on-new-security-case)
- International IDEA's tracker flagged concern about whistleblower protections in Israel in connection with the November 2025 arrest — [International IDEA](https://www.idea.int/democracytracker/report/israel/november-2025)
- Precedent cited by commentators: Anat Kam was sentenced to four and a half years for leaking documents implicating commanders in alleged war crimes, and Haaretz's Uri Blau faced possible charges under 113(c); senior journalists then argued prosecuting a reporter for holding secret material would upset the balance between "journalistic freedom, the freedom of expression and the need for security" (background only; covered by the other researcher) — [WSWS, 2011](https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2011/11/isra-n17.pdf); [Al Jazeera, 2010](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2010/4/8/israel-lifts-whistleblower-gag)

### Inferences
- Israel's leak-law architecture (censor pre-review, court gag orders, Penal Law secret-information offenses with no public-interest exception) means that "whistleblower" is a political and journalistic label in these cases, never a legal status; defendants have fought on intent, procedure and gag orders rather than on justification.
- Gag orders became a political battleground in 2024-2025: in the Feldstein and "Aleph" cases, government-aligned politicians themselves pushed to lift gags, reversing the usual alignment.

### Gaps
- Exact Penal Law section numbers for the Feldstein/Rosenfeld counts (likely sections 113(a)/(b) and 113(c)) and for "disclosure by a public servant" (likely section 117) could not be confirmed from the retrieved extracts; the student should check the Penal Law 5737-1977 text or the indictment coverage.
- No CPJ or ACRI report specifically on wartime gag orders was retrieved (search budget exhausted).
- No Israeli court decision in 2023-2026 addressing a whistleblower/public-interest defense on the merits was found; this is consistent with the absence of such a defense in Israeli law but should be stated as "none found" rather than "none exists."
- The 2025 censorship figures came from a search extract of +972's reporting and should be checked against the +972 article.
