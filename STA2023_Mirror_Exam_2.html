Question 4 (8 points): Reading Habits

According to a national survey, 35% of U.S. adults read at least one book last month. In a random sample of 8 U.S. adults, find the probability that:

  • (a) (2 pt) Exactly 2 read at least one book last month.
  • (b) (2 pt) At most 2 read at least one book last month.
  • (c) (2 pt) At least 1 read at least one book last month.
  • (d) (2 pt) For a random sample of 300 U.S. adults, find the expected number and standard deviation of those who read at least one book last month.

  • (a) (1 pt) Sketch or describe the graph of the uniform probability density function (pdf).
  • (b) (2 pt) Find the probability that the wait is between 5 and 15 minutes, i.e., \(P(5 \le X \le 15)\).
