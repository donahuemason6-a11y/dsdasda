## 7. Internships and the road to a job

Targets: state government, the legislature, and the courts. These hire in 1 to 3 months, want a bachelor's plus relevant experience, and do not require a security clearance. Odds of a related job within six months of graduation with two internships and a 3.3+ GPA: 75 to 85%.

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Fall 2026 | Enter the schedule in Pegasus Path. Join the pre-law society and the Political Science and Psychology student organizations. Start a résumé. |
| Spring 2027 | Research assistant in a clinical or forensic psychology lab. Apply for a Summer 2027 internship at the Orange-Osceola State Attorney's Office or the Public Defender's Office. |
| Summer 2027 | State Attorney or Public Defender internship, part-time, alongside 10 credits. |
| Fall 2027 | Intern in a state legislator's district office in Orlando. Apply to the Florida House or Senate intern program for a spring session if you are willing to spend January to March in Tallahassee with online sections. |
| Spring 2028 | Turn 18: register with Selective Service within 30 days. |
| Summer 2028 | Main internship: Ninth Judicial Circuit court administration or pretrial services, a state agency in Orlando (FDLE regional office, DCF, DJJ), or a county government analyst office. |
| Fall 2028 | Decide on graduate school. Law school: LSAT in Spring or Summer 2029 for Fall 2030 entry. Counseling, social work, or forensic psychology master's: applications due December 2029 to February 2030 for Fall 2030 entry. |
| Fall 2029 | Apply for state and court jobs 3 to 6 months out: Government Analyst and Operations and Management Consultant postings on People First, court program specialist and pretrial officer postings with the Ninth Circuit, legislative aide postings, victim advocate and investigator postings at State Attorney offices. |
| Spring 2030 | Graduate. Start work or graduate school in the summer or fall. |

**Jobs this plan qualifies you for on day one:** legislative aide, government or budget analyst in any state agency or county, court program specialist, pretrial services officer, probation officer (DOC or DJJ), State Attorney or Public Defender investigator or victim advocate, paralegal, crime intelligence analyst at FDLE or the Central Florida Intelligence Exchange. Entry pay in Florida runs $40k to $60k; Virginia, Maryland, Colorado, and Texas pay 20 to 40% more for the same titles.

**Graduate paths this plan sets up:** law school (prosecutor, public defender, legislative counsel), MPA (agency management), MSW or clinical counseling master's (court and corrections mental health), forensic psychology master's or doctorate (court evaluations). Graduating at 20 makes a graduate degree before 23 the natural move, and a 3.5+ GPA with a dual degree makes you a scholarship candidate.
