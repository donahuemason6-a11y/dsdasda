3. **POS 4407 counting** as a Prelaw restricted elective. Saves 3 credits. **Verify.**

Any one of the first two brings you to 105 or below.

## 5. Cost and funding

- In-state tuition and fees run about $212 per credit. Seven credits over is about $1,500; four over is about $850; zero if either lever in section 1 comes through.
- Excess-hours surcharge: depends on whether the Valencia hours were dual enrollment (**verify** with the Registrar). If they count, you are near the 144-hour baseline; if excluded, you are well under it.
- Bright Futures and Pell each cap funded hours. Confirm with Financial Aid which term, if any, falls outside the cap.
- Never withdraw from a course. Withdrawals burn paid credits and, if counted, excess-hours room.

## 7. Internships and the road to a job

Your targets are state government, the legislature, and the courts. These hire in 1 to 3 months, want a bachelor's plus relevant experience, and do not require a security clearance. Odds of a related job within six months of graduation with two internships and a 3.3+ GPA: 75 to 85%.

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Fall 2026 | Meet the ASC (section 6). Join UCF's pre-law society and the Political Science and Psychology student organizations. Start a résumé. |
| Spring 2027 | Volunteer as a research assistant in a clinical or forensic psychology lab. Apply for a Summer 2027 internship at the Orange-Osceola State Attorney's Office or the Public Defender's Office (both take undergraduates). |
| Summer 2027 | State Attorney or Public Defender internship, part-time, alongside the 13 credits. |
| Fall 2027 | Apply to the Florida House or Senate legislative intern program for the Spring 2028 session (Tallahassee, January to March; you would need online sections that term, or take it for credit through POS 4941 and shift Spring 2028 courses to summer). Turn 18 in 2028: register with Selective Service within 30 days. |
| Summer 2028 | Main internship: Ninth Judicial Circuit court administration or pretrial services, a state agency in Orlando (FDLE regional office, DCF, DJJ), or a county government analyst office. Light course load is planned for this. |
| Fall 2028 | Decide on graduate school. Law school: study for the LSAT and sit for it in Spring or Summer 2029 for Fall 2030 entry. Counseling, social work, or forensic psychology master's: applications are due December 2028 to February 2029 for Fall 2029 entry. |
| Spring 2029 | Apply for state and court jobs 3 to 6 months out: Government Analyst and Operations and Management Consultant postings on People First, court program specialist and pretrial officer postings with the Ninth Circuit, legislative aide postings, victim advocate and investigator postings at State Attorney offices. |
| Summer 2029 | Graduate. Start work or graduate school in August. |

**Jobs this plan qualifies you for on day one:** legislative aide, government or budget analyst in any state agency or county, court program specialist, pretrial services officer, probation officer (DOC or DJJ), State Attorney or Public Defender investigator or victim advocate, paralegal, crime intelligence analyst at FDLE or the Central Florida Intelligence Exchange. Entry pay in Florida runs $40k to $60k; Virginia, Maryland, Colorado, and Texas pay 20 to 40% more for the same titles.

**Graduate paths this plan sets up:** law school (prosecutor, public defender, legislative counsel), MPA (agency management), MSW or clinical counseling master's (court and corrections mental health), forensic psychology master's or doctorate (court evaluations). Graduating at 19 makes a graduate degree before 22 the natural move, and a 3.5+ GPA with a dual degree makes you a scholarship candidate.
