# UCF Registration Schedule

Political Science B.A. (Prelaw) + Psychology B.S. (Clinical) + Legal Studies minor. All declared 2026-09-24. 103 credits remaining after Fall 2026, 105 paid. Graduation May 2030. Full reasoning, requirement mapping, and prerequisite audit are in `UCF_Degree_Plan.md`.

## Before Spring 2027 registration (opens late October to early November 2026)

1. Clear every hold in myUCF (advising, immunization, financial).
2. Find your enrollment appointment time in myUCF (junior window with 79 hours).
3. Confirm myKnight Audit shows all three programs.
4. Load the Spring 2027 courses and backups into the shopping cart and run Validate.
5. Book the Spanish Placement Test at the University Testing Center (required before any Spanish enrollment).

| PSY 3024 | Nothing needed; offered every term |
| POS 3703 | CPO 3103 or POS 3424 |

General swap rule: any POS elective can trade terms with any other POS elective; any PLA elective with any other PLA elective; SOP 3004, PSB 3002, PPE 3003, PSB 3441, and DEP 2004 can move to any term. Do not move PSY 3204C, PSY 3213C, PSY 4604, or PSY 4049 out of order, and keep the two Spanish courses back to back.

## Standing rules

- C or better in every Psychology and Legal Studies course; 2.0 in Political Science; target 3.5 overall.
- No withdrawals.
- One internship every summer from 2027.
- Spanish Placement Test before Fall 2027 registration; Selective Service within 30 days of turning 18.
